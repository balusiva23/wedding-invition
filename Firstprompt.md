# MASTER ANTIGRAVITY PROMPT

## Create a Premium 3D South Indian Wedding Invitation Web App

Act as a **Senior UI/UX Designer, Creative Director, 3D Web Developer, Motion Designer, Frontend Architect, and Wedding Experience Designer**.

Build a completely modern, premium, immersive **3D South Indian Wedding Invitation website/web app**.

This must NOT look like a basic wedding invitation template.

The website should feel like:

**A cinematic wedding story + interactive digital invitation + South Indian cultural experience + modern 3D web application.**

The final result must be visually impressive enough to share through WhatsApp, Instagram, QR code, and mobile browsers.

---

Create an immersive wedding invitation experience where visitors enter the wedding story.
